PrepMate · Product design

Making interview preparation feel focused, private, and achievable.

Case study statusDesign direction in progressResearch and testing evidence can be added after validation.
01Problem statement

Placement preparation can feel fragmented when practice, feedback, presentation, and progress live in separate tools.

PrepMate brings these moments into one calm coaching experience, helping engineering students understand what to practise next without turning readiness into pressure.
